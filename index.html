Expertise

Process Intelligence

Fragmented data makes it hard to see where delays, rework and process deviations originate. Y/O Intelligence combines process mining, task mining and performance analysis to establish a reliable view of how the process performs, identify improvement opportunities and enable continuous performance monitoring.

Typical outputs: Process models, KPI baselines and monitoring, and a prioritized improvement backlog.

Intelligent Automation

Intelligent Automation reduces manual handoffs and makes processes run more reliably. Y/O Intelligence helps design and orchestrate workflows across people, systems, and automation tools.

Typical outputs: An automation assessment, an integrated workflow, or an implementation plan with clear dependencies and ownership.

AI & Agentic Systems

Y/O Intelligence applies AI and agentic systems within real business processes, grounded in reliable context and connected to the tools and systems needed to act. Solutions can support reasoning, decisions and task execution, with clear boundaries, evaluation and human oversight.

Typical outputs: A feasibility assessment, an evaluated prototype or an integration roadmap.

Data & Process Architecture

Reliable and scalable data models, connected business objects, clear process definitions and well-defined integrations provide the technical foundation for process intelligence, automation, AI and broader operational transformation.

02 /

Approach

  1. 01

    Understand

    Clarify the process, constraints and intended outcome. Establish what the available data can support.

  2. 02

    Prioritize

    Compare opportunities by value, effort and risk. Agree on a focused scope and how success will be assessed.

  3. 03

    Deliver

    Carry out the agreed analysis, design or implementation, with review points and documented decisions.

  4. 04

    Validate

    Check results against agreed criteria, document limitations, and define next steps for continuous improvement.

The right solution may be a process change, an analysis, an architecture decision, a roadmap, an automation or an AI pilot. The choice follows the evidence, not the technology.

Every engagement starts with a conversation to agree on scope, deliverables and ways of working.
